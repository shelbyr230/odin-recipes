Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish known for its rich, creamy texture—without using cream. It combines crispy pancetta, eggs, cheese, and black pepper to create a simple yet deeply flavorful sauce that coats every strand of pasta. Quick to prepare and made with just a few ingredients, it’s perfect for both weeknight dinners and impressing guests.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340g) spaghetti
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese (or Pecorino Romano for a more traditional flavor)
  • 4 oz (115g) pancetta or bacon, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt (for pasta water)
  • 1/4 cup reserved pasta water (as needed)

Steps

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add spaghetti and cook until al dente according to package instructions. Reserve about 1/4 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  2. Prepare the Sauce Base: In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, grated cheese, and black pepper until well combined. Set aside.
  3. Cook the Pancetta: In a large pan over medium heat, cook the diced pancetta (or bacon) until crispy. Add minced garlic (if using) and cook for about 30 seconds more.
  4. Combine Pasta and Pancetta: Add the drained spaghetti to the pan with pancetta. Toss to coat the pasta in the rendered fat. Remove the pan from heat.
  5. Create the Creamy Sauce: Slowly pour the egg and cheese mixture over the pasta, tossing quickly to coat. The residual heat will cook the eggs and create a creamy sauce. Add reserved pasta water a little at a time if needed to loosen the sauce.
  6. Serve Immediately: Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Serve hot with extra cheese and black pepper on top.
